Privacy & Cookies Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who I am

This website is run by Carolyn Davies trading as Mindful Communication, Impact Brixton, 17a Electric Lane, London SW9 8LA. I am the data controller for the personal information described here. You can contact me about your data at carolyn@mindfulcommunication.co.uk.

What I collect and why

Enquiries: when you use a form on this website, I receive your name, email address and anything you choose to tell me. I use this only to reply to you. The lawful basis is my legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Bookings: when you book a course, I receive your name, email address, ticket type and any details you give on the booking form. I use this to run the course, send joining details and keep business records. The lawful basis is to fulfil our contract.

Mailing list: if you sign up, I use your email address to send news about courses and events. The lawful basis is your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

Online courses: if you join an online course, your name and image may be visible to other participants on Zoom.

Services I use

I use trusted third parties to run this website and my courses. They process your data only on my behalf:

  • Formspree and Web3Forms (website forms)
  • Ticket Tailor and its payment providers (bookings and payments; I never see your card details)
  • Brevo (mailing list)
  • Zoom (online courses)
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting)

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries: for up to two years, unless they lead to a booking or ongoing work.
  • Booking and payment records: for six years, as required for tax purposes.
  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies or use tracking or advertising tools. The Ticket Tailor booking system may set cookies needed to process your booking. Like all website hosts, GitHub Pages receives your IP address when a page loads, and keeps it for security purposes.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal information I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how I use it, and to withdraw consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, email carolyn@mindfulcommunication.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

I never sell or share your data for marketing.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

If you cancel

Online trainings: I will offer a refund minus an £8 admin fee for cancellations up until 10 days before the course begins.

In-person trainings: I will offer a refund minus an £8 admin fee for cancellations up until 14 days before the course begins.

After that, I will issue a credit note valid for 1 year to attend another course in-person or online.

To cancel, email carolyn@mindfulcommunication.co.uk.

If I cancel

If I cancel an event, your full fee will be refunded. I accept no liability for refunding flights or accommodation costs.
